About

Bringing musicians together through diverse experiences

🎵

Monthly Community Jam Sessions

Large-scale open jams welcoming all musicians and audiences, hosted regularly across iconic public spaces and venues throughout the Bay Area. Free, family-friendly, and acoustic instruments only.

✨

Premium Musical Experiences

Going beyond traditional concerts — candlelight showcases, multi-sensory visual performances, intimate acoustic listening rooms, and curated venue takeovers.

🎙️

Studio Sessions

Professional recording sessions for unique covers and originals. Multi-take refined recordings with diverse genres and instruments. Released on our YouTube channel.

📚

Music Workshops

Educational sessions building technical mastery, soul, and emotion. Paid workshops and lessons for skill development across instruments and genres.

Our Mission

We respect tradition. We don't hide behind it. Concerts and Mehfils had their time. We're building what comes next — immersive, multi-sensory experiences built around music.

Raw sounds meet modern production. South Asian soul meets global ambition. Come learn, share, experiment. Come build with us.

SF Indian Music Project INC is a 501(c)(3) non-profit.
