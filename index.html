Who We Are

Modern Global Sound. South Asian Soul.

San Francisco Indian Music Project (SFIMP) is a non-profit, community-driven collective weaving music creation into the fabric of everyday life. We pioneer interactive musical experiences — finding the balance between raw traditional sounds and modern digital / AI production.

From public jam sessions across the Bay Area to candlelight showcases and studio recordings, our community brings musicians and music lovers together to learn, share, and experiment.

What We Do

Bringing musicians together through diverse experiences

🎵

Monthly Community Jam Sessions

Large-scale open jams welcoming all musicians and audiences, hosted regularly across iconic public spaces and venues throughout the Bay Area.

✨

Premium Musical Experiences

Going beyond traditional concerts, we build immersive musical experiences — including candlelight showcases, multi-sensory visual performances, intimate acoustic listening rooms, and curated venue takeovers.

🎙️

Studio Sessions

Professional recording sessions for unique covers and originals. Multi-take refined recordings with diverse genres and instruments.

📚

Music Workshops

Educational sessions building technical mastery, soul, and emotion. Paid workshops and lessons for skill development.
