SF Indian Music Project

San Francisco Indian Music Project

Global sound.
South Asian soul.

A 501c3 non-profit on a mission to weave music into the fabric of everyday life.

We are musicians building for musicians — tearing down the wall between audience and performer, making music something you live, not just watch.

We respect tradition. We don't hide behind it. Concerts and Mehfils had their time. We're building what comes next — immersive, multi-sensory experiences built around music.

Raw sounds meet modern production. South Asian soul meets global ambition.

Come learn, share, experiment. Come build with us.

What We Do

Bringing musicians together through diverse experiences

🎵

Community Jam Sessions

Large-scale open jams welcoming all musicians and audiences, hosted regularly across iconic public spaces and venues throughout the Bay Area.

✨

Premium Musical Experiences

Going beyond traditional concerts — candlelight showcases, multi-sensory visual performances, intimate acoustic listening rooms, and curated venue takeovers.

🎙️

Studio Sessions

Professional recording sessions for unique covers and originals. Multi-take refined recordings with diverse genres and instruments.

📚

Music Workshops

Educational sessions building technical mastery, soul, and emotion. Paid workshops and lessons for skill development.
